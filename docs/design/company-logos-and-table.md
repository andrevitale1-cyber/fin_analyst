# Company logo selection

Snapshot checked on 2026-10-12. This is a fixed visual selection, not a live ranking or a claim of partnership.

- S&P 500: https://www.slickcharts.com/sp500/marketcap — first 30 distinct companies; Alphabet's two share classes use one logo, so Caterpillar is included as the 30th distinct company.
- Brazil: https://www.companiesmarketcap.com/brazil/largest-companies-in-brazil-by-market-cap/ — first 10 companies in the site's Brazilian-company classification, including Nubank and JBS.
- Criterion: market capitalization, not revenue or index weight. Rankings change with market prices.
- Logos: company-logo WebP images served by CompaniesMarketCap, stored locally in frontend/public/benefits/company-logos. The precise selection is frontend/components/benefit-companies.json.

The landing page's aggregated table transcribes the eight complete rows visible in the user-supplied screenshot. It is a product preview, not current investment data. Column visibility and sorting only change the preview, not account data.
